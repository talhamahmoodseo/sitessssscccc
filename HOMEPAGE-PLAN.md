# Homepage — restore and improve

The original homepage is the foundation (owner rates it ~7/10). An earlier
rebuild that replaced it with a link-building-led structure was rejected:
it read like a service page, not an established agency with several services,
and it leaned on one person. This plan keeps what works and fixes what doesn't.

Design reference: amplemarket.com, for quality (spacing, grain, motion, polish,
hand-drawn touches). Our own branding stays: semibold headings with a grey
emphasis phrase, the amber highlighter, ink on warm paper.

Voice: an agency. "We" and "our team". No founder-led framing on the homepage.

## The Shift — plan

**What it says:** buyers ask one question in three kinds of places, and all
three lean on the same thing — what trusted sites say about you.

**Categories, named accurately:**

| Category | Examples | What the brand gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search results | Google, Bing | A ranked result |
| AI answers in search | Google AI Overviews, Perplexity | Cited as a source |
| AI assistants | ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini | Named as the recommendation |

ChatGPT and Claude are both assistants; they no longer sit next to Google as
if they were the same kind of thing.

**Heading:** "One question. Three places it gets answered." (keeps the
original concept: one question, many answers).

**Visual:** a query at the top, three category cards that each show a short,
natural answer, and one base bar — "Authority links & mentions on the
publications all three trust". Dashed connectors draw in; small dots travel
up from the base into each card; the cards take turns being active.

**Numbers:** −61% informational CTR and +18.7% branded queries, sourced to
Neil Patel, with his quote set as a compact, readable pull quote.

## Process — plan

Five steps that fit every service, not just link building: **Audit → Plan →
Vet → Create & place → Track & report**. The pinned scroll, rolling step
number and progress rail stay; the rail moves to brand colours (ink + amber)
instead of bright green, and each step gets its own small visual instead of a
single "how a link travels" loop.

## Rules kept

- No red accent; pastels only in small accents and stat tiles.
- Text never below 70% ink.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Sample proof stays marked as sample until real material arrives.
