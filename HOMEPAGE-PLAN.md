# Homepage — content and design plan

Written before the rebuild. Content, layout, visuals and motion were planned
together. Amplemarket's homepage is the design reference; it was studied live
(13 viewport captures plus computed styles) before any of this was written.

---

## 1. What Amplemarket actually does

Measured, not remembered:

| Element | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Labil Grotesk, **regular weight (400)**, 56px H1, −0.05em tracking, line-height 1.0. A few letters or words set in italic inside headlines. Geist Mono for small labels. |
| Ground | Warm off-white `rgb(246,245,243)`, near-black `#111` text. Almost identical to ours. |
| Colour | Big, saturated, **grainy** orange and lavender clouds in the hero only. Pastel tiles in the results grid. A few full-dark sections. Colour never washes a whole light section. |
| Hero | Centred. "NEW" pill, headline, one-line sub, email field with a ratings tray tucked under it, hand-drawn sketch figure (rocket), large product UI. |
| Rhythm | Logo grid → big single quote → dark product section with floating note cards → dark "signals" marquee → stacked feature cards ("click me!") → quote → bold CTA panel → persona tabs with pencil figures → pastel results bento ("see the insights!") → blog cards → dark testimonial wall → dark final CTA → footer. |
| Voice of proof | A single customer quote sits between sections, again and again. Proof is spread through the page, not parked in one place. |
| Handmade touches | Handwritten notes with drawn arrows; pencil illustrations. These stop it feeling like a template. |

**The owner's direction, on top of all this:** we are a premium link
building agency, not a software product. Amplemarket is the reference for
*design quality* — type, spacing, colour, motion, handmade touches — never for
its SaaS patterns or its words. Every line of copy here is our own.

What we take: regular-weight display type with italic accents, the warm paper
ground, grainy colour clouds in the hero only, hand-drawn figures and
handwritten notes, a single big client quote between sections, pastel results
bento, a dark testimonial wall, generous spacing, motion that explains.

What we leave: product dashboards and app screenshots, "free trial" email
fields, notification-chip marquees, feature tabs full of software UI, the
uppercase "super sales" CTA. Those make a site read as software. An agency
shows **its work, its people and its results** instead.

Font: Labil Grotesk is a paid licence, so Plus Jakarta Sans is set the same
way (lighter weight, tight tracking, italic accents). The whole site can switch
later if the licence is bought.

## 2. Positioning

The owner's direction: search is moving past ten blue links. Businesses now
want to be **discovered, mentioned and recommended** by AI systems as well as
ranked by Google. We should sound like an agency built for where search is
going, while **link building stays at the centre** of what we sell. Not
another Google-rankings shop, not an "AI agency" either.

The idea that holds it together:

> Buyers find brands in three places now: Google's results, the sites they
> read, and AI answers. All three lean on the same thing: what trusted
> websites say about you. A good editorial link or mention feeds all three at
> once. That's why link building is more valuable now, not less.

The line: **Get found. Get mentioned. Get recommended.** Found = Google,
mentioned = trusted publications and their readers, recommended = AI answers.
No GEO/AEO jargon on the page except once, in the FAQ, in plain words.

## 3. What the homepage has to do

1. **Say who we are in five seconds.**
2. **Explain why link building matters more now**, without lecturing.
3. **Earn trust**: real work, real results, a real person.
4. **Route people** to the right service, or straight to a call.

Removed: video testimonials (owner's request), the long process section
(lives on service pages), the separate contact form, duplicated proof
sections, the standalone "who we are" paragraph (its points now live in the
founder note and How we work), and anything that reads like a software product.

## 5. CTA rhythm

Hero buttons → featured link building CTA → mid-page visibility audit → founder "book
a call with me" → final CTA. Every CTA leads to the same booking flow.

## 6. Rules kept

- No red accent (rust only marks a rejection).
- Pastels only in stat tiles and the selected persona glow.
- Text never below 70% ink.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Placeholder proof (names, numbers, quotes) is sample content for design
  review. Replace before launch.
