Status: **built (round 9).** The owner's answers are in section 8. Every
number on the case studies is listed in `CASE-STUDY-DRAFTS.md`.

## 1. What went wrong in round 8

- Strong sections were **deleted** instead of improved: the featured review,
  the "see the numbers / Results our clients take to their boards" proof grid,
  the review wall, the case-study report layout, and the four-plan pricing
  page with its comparison table.
- The goal (no invented content) was right, but the method was wrong. The
  designs should have stayed, with real clients and approved drafts put into
  them.
- The process section was replaced with a generic timeline. The problem was
  never the pinned scroll idea. It was that each step showed only a big
  number and a line of text, so it looked low-resolution next to sites like
  Amplemarket.

   CapCut, Defender and the agency partner (Tiffany Lei). CryptoDesk is
