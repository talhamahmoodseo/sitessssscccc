## 1. Ground rules (read once)

1. **One name, everywhere:** `Editorial HQ`. Never "EditorialHQ", "Editorial H.Q." or "Editorial HQ Agency". Search engines and AI tools match profiles to your website by the exact name, website and logo. If these differ from profile to profile, Google treats them as different businesses.
2. **One handle, everywhere:** `editorialhq`.
   - As of 29 Oct 2026, LinkedIn (`/company/editorialhq`), X and YouTube (`@editorialhq`) returned "not found", so the handle looks free there.
   - Instagram, Facebook, Threads and TikTok hide this behind a login, so check those yourself.
   - If it's taken, use the fallbacks in this order: `editorialhq.co` → `editorial.hq` → `editorialhqagency` → `geteditorialhq`. Use the same fallback on every platform where you need one.
3. **One website link:** `https://editorialhq.com`, always with https and no www. Add a UTM tag so GA4 shows which profile sends visitors. For example:
   `https://editorialhq.com/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=profile`
   Change `utm_source` per platform: `instagram`, `x`, `clutch`, `trustpilot`, `crunchbase`, `goodfirms`, `designrush`, `sortlist`.
   **Exception:** on Clutch, Crunchbase, GoodFirms and DesignRush, use the clean URL without UTM. Those profile links also count as backlinks, so keep them clean.
4. **One email for sign-ups:** `team@editorialhq.com`. A company email verifies the domain on Trustpilot, LinkedIn and Clutch automatically.
5. **Keep freelancing separate.** No Fiverr, Upwork, Legiit or ComeUp links on any Editorial HQ profile. The brand is an agency, and your personal profiles can stay as they are.
6. **Security:** turn on two-factor login everywhere. Keep logins in a password manager (Bitwarden is free) and share the vault with your team member, never the passwords in chat.

**Rules (break these and Trustpilot flags or penalises the page):**

- Don't only ask the happy clients ("review gating"). Invite every client after delivery.

- Reply to every review, good or bad, within 48 hours. Keep it short and specific.

## 6. Tips that actually move the needle
1. **Consistency beats volume.** Ten identical profiles do more for brand search than twenty half-done ones.
2. **The founder link matters.** Talha Mahmood → Founder → Editorial HQ, the same on LinkedIn, Crunchbase and the website. AI tools build their picture of a company from these connections.
3. **Reviews > profiles.** A Clutch page with 5 detailed reviews outsells any description. Spend your energy there.
4. **Search your brand name every month,** on Google and in ChatGPT/Perplexity, with "Editorial HQ link building". Wherever the wrong info shows up, fix that profile first.
5. **Use real numbers only:** the client results already approved for the website. A made-up stat on Clutch can get the profile flagged.
6. **Your team member** can do the directory listings and review invites from this doc. Your time goes on LinkedIn content and client calls.
