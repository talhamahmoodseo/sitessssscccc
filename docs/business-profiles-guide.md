# Editorial HQ: profiles that sell (v3)

How to set up every Editorial HQ profile the way an established agency
would. It covers what each profile says, where client proof goes, and the
direct pitch. Every line below is ready to copy.

**What changed in v3:** v2 put one client and one ranking in the bios, the
headline and the descriptions. That makes the agency look built around a
single success story. In v3, **profiles say who we are and what we offer**,
and **client results live in the places buyers go looking for proof**:
highlights, featured posts, portfolios and case studies, with different
clients shown in different places.

---

## 1. The rule: identity up front, proof one click away

| Where | What goes there | What doesn't |
|---|---|---|
| **Bios, headlines, taglines, "About" openings** | Who we are, what we offer, the value, the next step | Client names, rankings, traffic numbers |
| **Long descriptions** (LinkedIn About, Clutch, Crunchbase) | Positioning, how we work, guarantees. At most **one** "Clients include…" line, near the end | Any one client's results as the headline |
| **Highlights, Featured, pinned posts, portfolios, case studies** | Client results: screenshots, rankings, traffic, placements, quotes | The same client in every slot |
| **Posts** | Mostly expertise, sometimes proof, with a different client each time | One client repeated week after week |

Think of it like a good agency office: the sign on the door says what the
company does, and the case studies are on the wall inside.

### The 5-second test (bios and headers)

A visitor must answer three questions in five seconds:

1. **What is this?** → "Link building & digital PR agency"
2. **Why them?** → "Editorial links on real publications, checked by hand"
3. **What do I do next?** → "3 free backlinks for qualified brands" (or "Book a call")

The proof (*does it work?*) is the next thing they see: highlights, the
featured section, a pinned post.

- The same layout every time: ink background, white text, the brand font.

**Highlight covers:** ink circles with one white line icon each (chart,
arrow-up, document, sparkle, quote, gift, person).
