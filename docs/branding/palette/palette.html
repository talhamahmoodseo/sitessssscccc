EditorialHQ social palette

Ink and paper, with one editor's red. Two neutrals do the work; the red only marks what matters.

Ink#0E0E0EBackgrounds of banners and covers, headlines on light posts
Paper#F7F6F4 (and white #FFFFFF)Light backgrounds, text on Ink
Editor's Red#E25428One mark per design: a word, a strike, a number

Rough share in any design: about 55% Ink, 37% Paper, 8% Red. Warm grey #8C8A86 for small secondary text only.

Do

Use red once per design: the key word, an editor's strike, or the one number that matters. Keep everything else Ink and Paper. Client logos always in one tone (white on Ink, black on Paper). Photos with neutral backgrounds.

Don't

No gradients, no second accent colour, no coloured glows, no pastel tiles on social. No red body text or red backgrounds behind long text. Never more than three colours in one design.
