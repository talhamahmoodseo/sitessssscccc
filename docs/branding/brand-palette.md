# EditorialHQ social palette

**Decision:** Ink and Paper, with one accent, Editor's Red.

| Name | Hex | Role | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0E0E0E` | Banner and cover backgrounds, headlines on light posts | ~55% |
| Paper | `#F7F6F4` (white `#FFFFFF` allowed) | Light backgrounds, text on Ink | ~37% |
| Editor's Red | `#E25428` | One mark per design: a key word, an editor's strike, one number | ~8% |
| Warm grey | `#8C8A86` | Small secondary text only (labels, captions) | Text only |

Swatches and rules: `palette/palette.png`.

## Why this direction

- **It's the business, made visible.** Editors mark copy in red. Your work
  is editorial (real publications, real editors), and the strike-through
  you like is literally an editor's red line. The colour carries the idea.
- **Premium and established.** Black, off-white and one confident accent is
  the language of serious publishers and top agencies. It reads as
  confident and calm, not cheap or "designed by a template".
- **Distinctive in SEO.** Most link building and SEO agencies use blue,
  purple or green. A red-orange accent on ink stands out in a feed full of
  them.
- **Already yours.** `#E25428` is the warm end of the Editorial HQ logo, so
  the social look and the logo stay connected.
- **Works small.** Black and off-white give maximum contrast at banner and
  thumbnail size, and one accent can't clutter.
- **Easy to keep consistent.** Three colours and one rule ("red once per
  design") work for banners, carousels, quote cards, post images and
  slides, without new colours ever creeping in.

## Rules

**Do**
- Use red once per design, on the thing you want remembered.
- Keep everything else Ink and Paper.
- Show client logos in one tone: white on Ink, black on Paper.
- Use photos with neutral backgrounds.

**Don't**
- No gradients, glows, second accent colours or pastel tiles on social.
- No red body text, no red behind long text.
- Never more than three colours in one design.

## Follow-ups this decision implies

1. **Profile photo background:** the mint background is now the only colour
   outside the palette. Regenerate it in a neutral warm grey or Paper (one
   follow-up prompt: "Change only the background to a plain warm off-white,
   #F7F6F4. Keep me exactly the same.").
2. **Logo for social:** a one-colour version (Ink, Paper, or with the red
   symbol) instead of the orange-to-violet gradient. To make next.
3. **Website (later, only with the owner's approval):** the site's accent is
   the orange-to-violet gradient plus pastel tiles. Moving the website's
   accent to Editor's Red would make site and social match. Not changed yet.
