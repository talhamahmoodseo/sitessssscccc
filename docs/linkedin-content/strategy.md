# LinkedIn content strategy (working notes, before any skill)

Status: draft strategy, waiting for the owner's feedback. Nothing here is a
skill yet. Skills get written only from approved posts and feedback.

## Inputs studied
- Lara Acosta's YouTube video (transcript): 6 rules. 1) Never start from a
  blank page (adapt outlier posts' hook + structure, not words). 2) Tell stories
  then teach: SLAY = Story, Lesson, Actionable advice, You-question. 3) Profile
  is a landing page. 4) Comments are mini posts (30–60 min a day, comment on
  your own post too). 5) Stand next to people your audience trusts (reference
  them, lives, podcasts). 6) Offer: specific, clear transformation, a named
  3–5 step path, genuine urgency only.
- Five recent Lara posts: audience-choice post, 365-day roadmap + graphic,
  "10+ accounts viral" proof + named people + infographic, "100+ creators"
  list + comment-to-get, origin story ("outsider", photo of herself).

## Owner's situation
- ~1,400 followers, no posts for about a year, profile fully optimised.
- Sells link building / digital PR. Buyers: SEO agencies (white-label),
  SaaS, brands. Pricing is discussed in chat on LinkedIn and Upwork; fixed
  packages are for the website only.
- Assets: SmileTutor (#1 for "tuition", Singapore), CryptoDesk (#1 for
  "crypto exchange in dubai" in 5 months), rejected-site examples, his own
  AI-built tools, a small team, Faisalabad-to-global journey.

## Decisions (proposed)
- Restart with a comeback post, no pitch.
- Two kinds of posts: trust posts (buyers: proof, link quality) and reach
  posts (journey, AI building). Roughly 2 trust : 1 reach.
- Pillars: proof stories, the dirty side of link building, building with AI,
  the journey.
- Every post uses SLAY and has a story, proof or a number.
- Daily: 30–45 min of real comments on agency owners' and SEO creators' posts.
- Formats: text + photo of Talha for stories, text + screenshot for proof,
  carousel for frameworks.

## Round 2 feedback (owner) + audience data
- Followers: 46% entry level, 17% SEO specialists, 14% Lahore; founders/CEOs
  about 10%. So the audience is mostly Pakistani SEO peers; buyers are a small
  share. Posts must be written for practitioners to engage with and buyers to
  trust (practitioner-level link building insight), and buyers are reached
  through comments and DMs.
- Rule: no client names, URLs or identifying details. Never claim sole credit:
  "I helped", "the links I built were one part of it".
- Old posts that worked (2 years ago): playful hook ("I am gonna give you a
  KISS"), short lines, arrow bullets, P.S. question. Weakness: generic tips with
  no story or proof. Keep the voice, add story + proof.

## Round 3: playbook-based strategy (Lara Acosta playbook PDF)
- Owner feedback: link-builder insider posts (disappearing links, reseller
  prices, DR pricing) only reach other link builders, not clients. Max 1–2 a
  month, framed for buyers.
- Category of 1: brands found on Google and recommended by AI through mentions
  on sites people read. Link building is the mechanism, not the topic.
- 4-3-2-1: Tue Educate (PAS/framework, "How I"), Wed Document (proof, build in
  public), Thu Storytell (SLAY/POV, broad, candid photo), Sat Sell (value
  bridge, halo, customer-eyes, end-to-end). 80% educational / 20% personal.
- Signature series: AI Visibility Teardown. Named framework: 3R Test.
  Lead magnet: AI Visibility Prompt Pack. Core offer: free link audit.
