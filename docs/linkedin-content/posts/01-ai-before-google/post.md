## Caption

Your next client might never Google you.

They'll open ChatGPT and type:
"Who's the best [what you sell] for [their problem]?"

If your name isn't in that answer, you're not on the shortlist.

And here's the part that hurts:
You won't see it anywhere.
No click. No impression. Nothing in Google Analytics.
You lose the lead and never find out.

Most brands have never checked this once.

So here's how I check if AI knows a brand.
5 prompts. 10 minutes. Slides below.

The one that matters most is the last one:
"Which sources did you use for this answer?"

Because AI doesn't invent its shortlist.
It repeats what trusted sites say about your category.

If those sites mention your competitor and not you,
you'll keep losing deals you never knew existed.

Run prompt 2 for your brand today.
Were you on the list?
