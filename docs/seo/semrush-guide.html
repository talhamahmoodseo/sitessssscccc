Semrush keyword research for Editorial HQ

For Muse. Follow this step by step. Your job is to collect the raw keyword data from Semrush and export it. You don't need to clean it, group it or decide anything. Claude will turn your exports into the topic clusters and content plan, and Talha will approve it.

The most important rule: export more, filter less. If you're unsure whether a keyword fits, keep it. Claude removes the bad ones. A missing keyword can't be recovered later.

How the whole job works

  1. Muse (this guide): runs 5 tasks in Semrush and exports CSV files into one Google Drive folder. About 3–4 hours.
  2. Claude: cleans the data, removes junk, groups keywords into topic clusters, picks the primary and secondary keyword for every page, and decides which pages are service pages and which are blog posts.
  3. Talha: reviews and approves the plan.
  4. Claude: writes the pages and builds them on the website.

Before you start

  • Semrush menus move around. If you can't find a tool in the left menu, type its name in the search bar at the top of Semrush.
  • Database (country): each tool has a country selector next to the search box. Use US unless a task says otherwise. Our clients are worldwide, and US data is the biggest English dataset.
  • Exporting: every table has an Export button at the top right. Choose CSV and all rows (not just the current page).
  • File names: use the names given in each task. Example: B-white-label-link-building-US.csv.
  • Put everything in one Google Drive folder called Semrush exports Nov 2026 and share it with Talha.

Task A: What our competitors rank for

This shows us which keywords already bring traffic to link building agencies, and which of their pages get it.

First, find the competitors

  1. Go to SEO → Keyword Research → Keyword Overview. Database: US.
  2. Search link building services.
  3. Scroll to SERP Analysis (the list of pages ranking on Google). Write down the domains of the top 10 results.
  4. Do the same for digital pr agency and white label link building.
  5. Skip Wikipedia, Reddit, YouTube, Forbes-style lists and directories (Clutch, G2, Fiverr, Upwork). We only want agencies that sell what we sell.

Also include these if they aren't already on your list. Check that each site is a link building or digital PR agency before using it:

editorial.link · outreachmonks.com · fatjoe.com · loganix.com · userp.io · rhinorank.io · linkbuilder.io · thehoth.com

Pick the 10 best competitors: the closest to us, meaning they sell link building or digital PR as a service.

Then export each competitor's keywords

  1. Go to SEO → Competitive Research → Organic Research. Database: US.
  2. Enter a competitor domain. Open the Positions tab.
  3. Set the filters:
    • Positions → Top 20
    • Volume → from 10
  4. Export: A-positions-[competitor].csv
  5. Open the Pages tab (their pages that bring the most traffic). Export without filters: A-pages-[competitor].csv
  6. Repeat for all 10 competitors.

Task B: Keywords they have and we don't

  1. Go to SEO → Competitive Research → Keyword Gap. Database: US.
  2. Put editorialhq.com in the first box and the 4 strongest competitors from Task A in the other boxes (the ones with the most traffic).
  3. Click Compare.
  4. In the table, open the Missing tab and export it: B-gap-missing-set1.csv
  5. Open the Untapped tab and export it: B-gap-untapped-set1.csv
  6. Run it again with the next 4 competitors: B-gap-missing-set2.csv and B-gap-untapped-set2.csv

Task C: Every keyword around our services

This is the biggest task. For each seed keyword below, you collect all related keywords.

  1. Go to SEO → Keyword Research → Keyword Magic Tool.
  2. Type the seed keyword. Database: US. Click Search.
  3. Keep the match type on Broad Match (the default).
  4. Use only these filters:
    • Volume → from 10. Our buyers search in small numbers, so a 20-search keyword can be worth $1,000+.
    • Advanced filters → Exclude keywords → paste the exclude list below.
  5. Don't filter by KD% (difficulty) or intent. Claude will do that.
  6. Export all rows: C-[seed]-US.csv
  7. For the seeds marked ★, run them again in the UK, Australia and UAE databases: C-[seed]-UK.csv, C-[seed]-AU.csv, C-[seed]-AE.csv

What to keep and what to skip

Keep

  • Anything someone would search before hiring a link builder or PR agency
  • Low-volume buyer keywords (10–50 a month is fine)
  • Price and cost searches ("link building cost", "how much do backlinks cost")
  • Industry keywords (SaaS, fintech, ecommerce, crypto, local)
  • Country keywords (UK, Dubai, Australia, USA)
  • Comparison and "best" keywords ("best link building services")
  • How-to questions about links, PR and AI search

Skip (already handled by the exclude list or easy to spot)

  • Free tools ("free backlinks", "backlink checker", "backlink generator")
  • Jobs, salaries and courses
  • Competitor brand searches on their own ("fatjoe login")
  • Other languages and spammy "1000 backlinks for $5" searches
  • Anything about software you can't hire us for

When in doubt, keep it. Claude will remove it.
