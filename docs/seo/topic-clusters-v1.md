difficulty, and the final primary keyword can change.

- Blog size: Editorial.Link 119 posts, LinkBuilder.io 165, FATJOE 193, Siege
  Media 441, Outreach Monks 497, uSERP 522. Editorial HQ has 6. We can't
  out-publish them. We win with depth, real proof and clusters where they're thin.
- Every one of them has separate pages for: white-label link building, guest
  posting, niche edits / link insertion, link building packages / pricing,
  digital PR, brand mentions, AI / GEO services.
- Industry pages are standard: SaaS, ecommerce, law firms, fintech, healthcare,
  real estate, startups, enterprise (uSERP, Outreach Monks, Siege, Page One Power).
- "Best X" listicles drive buyer traffic: best link building services / agencies,
  for ecommerce / fintech / healthcare / startups, best guest posting services,
  best FATJOE alternatives, best AEO/GEO agencies (Outreach Monks, LinkBuilder.io).
- Page One Power runs comparison pages ("uSERP vs Page One Power", "FATJOE vs
  Page One Power") and a 100+ term glossary.
- AI search is the newest race: uSERP has about 77 AI/GEO posts, Siege 64 and a
  GEO page per industry. Editorial.Link and LinkBuilder.io are still thin there.
- Free tools as link magnets: FATJOE (about 20 tools), LinkBuilder.io (AI report card),
  Editorial.Link (link building statistics page).
- HARO: Editorial.Link and LinkBuilder.io each have "HARO link building" and
  "HARO alternatives". Almost nobody owns HARO as a service cluster. That's an opening.

## Publishing pace (counter to "10 a week")
- Month 1: 6 service/industry pages + 8 blog posts.
- From month 2: 3–4 blog posts a week.

Why: scaled AI content without real examples falls under Google's scaled-content
abuse policy, and a new domain earns trust slowly. Every page must include one real
example, number or screenshot from Talha's work. That's the edge over 500-post blogs.
