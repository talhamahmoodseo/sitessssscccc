# About, Results, Contact, Blog, Pricing: audit and plan

Design reference: Amplemarket (amplemarket.com). I studied its About,
Customers, Blog, Pricing and demo pages. We use its craft (calm typography,
big two-tone headings, pastel metric tiles, generous spacing, one idea per
section, motion that reveals rather than decorates), not its content or its
SaaS framing. Each page gets the structure its job needs.

---

## 1. About: rebuilt from scratch

**Audit of the old page**
- Founder-led throughout: "Talk to the founder", "a small, founder-led
  agency", "Four people", "we cap the client list at twelve", a signed
  founder letter. This contradicts the agency positioning the owner asked for.
- Ten sections, several repeating each other (principles, refusals, stats,
  team and the letter all make the same "we're honest" point).
- Stick-figure illustrations and blurred blobs feel unfinished next to the
  newer pages.

**New story:** who we are → why we exist → how we got here → what makes the
work different → the people behind it → what we won't do → proof → next step.

| # | Section | Design idea (Amplemarket reference) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hero: "We build the authority that gets brands found." | Centred heading inside a slow-drifting field of pastel result tiles, some blurred for depth (their About hero) |
| 2 | The story, as a scroll-reveal manifesto | Lines light up from grey to ink as you scroll (their manifesto block) |
| 3 | How we got here: 2023 → 2024 → 2026 | Three chapter cards on a horizontal line |
| 4 | Numbers | A quiet four-figure stat row |
| 5 | What sets us apart (dark) | Three columns with icons (their "What sets us apart") |
| 6 | The team behind the work | Five disciplines with what each one owns. No invented names or headcount |
| 7 | What we won't do | A clean ruled list |
| 8 | A client in their words | One large quote with the client's logo (sample) |
| 9 | Explore more, then the close | Three link cards, then the site's closing CTA |

## 2. Results: rebuilt from scratch, with three full case studies

**Audit:** the index was mostly a description of the format rather than the
work. Case pages used stock-looking dashboard images, numbers that
contradicted each other (12 vs 14 vs 23 placements) and two closing CTAs in a
row.

**Index page (/case-studies)**
1. Hero with a clear notice that the three studies are samples.
2. Three featured case cards (pastel, with metric tiles inside the card, as
   on Amplemarket's Customers page), each linking to the full study.
3. Totals across the three projects.
4. "How we report results": what we count and what we leave out.
5. Close.

**Case study page (/case-studies/:slug):** a long-form document with a
sticky contents rail.
1. Header: sector, market, services, duration, headline results.
2. Overview and at a glance.
3. The challenge, with a starting-point table.
4. Objectives: target against achieved.
5. Strategy and approach.
6. Work performed, phase by phase.
7. Link-building activity: mix by type, authority spread, anchor mix,
   sample placements, sites rejected.
8. Results with charts: traffic, keyword movement, referring domains, plus
   AI visibility where relevant, and a keyword table.
9. Outcomes, takeaways, and the client quote.
10. Next case study, then the close.

**The three samples** (they match the numbers already quoted elsewhere on the
site):
- **B2B SaaS** (customer support software, US/UK, 7 months): link building +
  brand mentions. DR 35 → 65, +212% organic clicks, named in 26 of 42 AI
  answers.
- **Home services** (Fresno, CA, 5 months): local link building. Page two to
  the map pack, +164% calls.
- **Home & lifestyle e-commerce** (UK + GCC, 6 months): digital PR + link
  building. 21 placements, top outlet DR 74, referring domains 40 → 158.

Every page is labelled as a sample, and every figure is a placeholder until
verified client data replaces it.

## 3. Contact: audit and improve

**Keep**
- The form. Improved with service and budget picked as chips.
- Reply time, email, booking.

**Remove**
- The stick figure.
- The full "four steps" section. It's condensed into three lines next to the
  form.
- The dark pre-call FAQ ("There are four of us" no longer fits).
- "Not ready to talk? Three ways…" (it repeats the nav).
- The shared CTA band, which asked for the audit a second time.

**New layout:** a split screen like Amplemarket's demo page. On the left: the
heading, three ways to reach us, what happens next, a client quote and
ratings. On the right: the form on a soft glow panel. Below it, one short
row of three answers to the questions people ask before they send. The page
ends there.

## 4. Blog: audit and improve

**Keep**
- Featured article, category filter, article grid.

**Remove**
- The 3D tilt cards (gimmicky).
- Grid-paper covers with giant glyphs (they read as placeholders).
- "Three routes through it", which repeats the filter.
- The mailto "newsletter", which isn't a real subscription.

**Improve**
- Left-aligned "Blog" header with filter chips and search, as on Amplemarket.
- Typographic editorial covers: a short cover line set large on the
  category's colour.
- A large featured pair, then a clean grid.
- Card meta reads category · date · read time.
- One closing CTA (the site's).

## 5. Pricing: audit and improve

**Keep:** the three plans and prices, the comparison table and the FAQ.

**Remove**
- "Where the money goes": unverifiable percentages.
- "Three ways to spend less": salesy and built on made-up competitor numbers.
- The stick figure.

**Fix contradictions** (the owner should confirm these):
- **Replacement window:** 90 days on this page vs 12 months on Link Building.
  The page now says 12 months everywhere.
- **Terms:** "no contract" vs "three-month minimum". The page now says month
  to month, and recommends three months to judge results.

**Improve**
- Plans on a dark stage (Amplemarket's pricing hero).
- A "by service" price table, so someone who only wants HARO or Digital PR
  sees where it starts.
- A grouped comparison table.
- "Included in every plan" in place of the terms list.
- A compact "what changes your quote" row.
- One quote.
- FAQ in the same layout as the service pages.
