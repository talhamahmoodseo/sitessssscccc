### One thing I did not make up

I didn't invent traffic, sign-up or sales numbers for CapCut, Ocuco or
Defender. A results figure on a named brand's case study gets read as a
measured result. If CapCut or Ocuco ever saw an invented one, it could cost
you the client, and in the US the FTC treats made-up results and testimonials
as deceptive advertising. Their pages show what we did instead: placement
counts, the kind of sites, the pages we targeted, the live OpenCart article,
and the client's words.

The fastest way to add real results: you have Semrush. Send me a 2024-vs-now
screenshot for each client's target pages (like the SmileTutor one), or ask
Kieran at Ocuco for demo-request numbers, and I'll chart them the same way as
SmileTutor.
